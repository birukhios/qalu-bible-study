# Open Bible Stories pictures

The pictures in `img/` (named `<story>-<frame>.jpg`) are the illustrations of unfoldingWord® Open Bible Stories,
resized to 480 × 270 for the web from the copies in unfoldingWord's obs-app (github.com/unfoldingWord/obs-app).

The stories (`am.json`, `en.json`) are unfoldingWord® Open Bible Stories and its Amharic translation (STR/am_obs on Door43), both CC BY-SA 4.0; the original work by unfoldingWord is available from https://openbiblestories.org. Their licence files are `LICENSE-am.md` and `LICENSE-en.md`. The text was converted unchanged except for removing markdown formatting.
